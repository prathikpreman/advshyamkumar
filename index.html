About

A trial lawyer clients turn to when the stakes are highest

Adv. Shyam Kumar MP is a criminal lawyer practising before the High Court of Kerala, with over 10 years of courtroom experience defending clients across the state. His practice covers bail and anticipatory bail applications, criminal trials, appeals, revisions, and quashment petitions under the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita.

He has appeared in several closely watched and widely reported cases across Kerala, earning a reputation for meticulous case preparation, firm advocacy, and calm judgment under pressure. Clients engage him at every stage — from the first FIR to the final verdict — for advice that is direct, strategic, and built on a thorough command of criminal procedure.
